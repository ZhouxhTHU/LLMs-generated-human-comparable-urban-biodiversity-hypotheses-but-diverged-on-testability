Public release: human–LLM expert evaluation

Scope
This package contains only materials supporting the article version that reports inter-observer agreement across all repeatedly rated hypothesis-pair types: human–human, human–LLM and LLM–LLM.
The separate cross-source-only reliability analysis is not included.

Contents
data: source comparison workbook used by the shared analyses.
code: BTL model, Bayesian summary, all-pairs agreement and final-figure scripts.
results/BTL_model: posterior samples, summaries and diagnostics from the BTL model.
results/inter_observer_all_pairs: all-source repeated-pair agreement results.
figures: final four-panel figure and supporting BTL and agreement figures.

Internal reports and manuscript drafting materials are intentionally excluded from this public release.
